Profile

I am a PhD candidate at the QUT Centre for Robotics and CSIRO Robotics, specialising in soft robotics, robotic manipulation, grasping, benchmarking, and evaluation of robotic systems. My research focuses on developing novel soft robotic grippers and tactile sensing technologies to enable safe and effective manipulation of common hand tools and fragile objects in real-world environments.
